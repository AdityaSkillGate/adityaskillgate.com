General Eligibility

  • Graduates from any recognized university (B.E, B.Tech, B.Sc, BCA, B.Com, B.A).
  • Final year students awaiting results are eligible for provisional admission.
  • Working professionals looking to upskill or transition to IT.
  • No prior coding experience is required for foundation courses.

Required Documents

Please keep the following documents ready (digital copies) before starting your application:

  • Latest Resume / CV
  • Recent passport size photograph
  • Government ID Proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
  • Degree Certificate or Final Year Marksheets

The Admission Process

1. Apply Online

Submit your application form and documents securely.

2. Profile Review

Our admissions team will evaluate your eligibility.

3. Counseling

1-on-1 session to align your career goals with the programme.

4. Enrollment

Receive your offer letter and join the upcoming batch.
