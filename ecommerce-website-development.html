Frequently Asked Questions

E-commerce Development FAQs

Clear answers regarding platform selection, payment setup, timelines, and maintenance.

How do I choose between Shopify, WooCommerce, and a Custom web store?
Choose Shopify if you want a fast, low-maintenance direct-to-consumer (D2C) store with managed cloud hosting. Choose WooCommerce if you already use WordPress and want complete database ownership with zero platform transaction fees. Choose Custom Full-Stack (React/Node.js) if you require proprietary business workflows, complex B2B pricing, or extreme speed with heavy catalog volume.
What payment gateways can you integrate for Indian and international customers?
We integrate all leading Indian and international payment gateways including Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, Stripe, and PayPal, supporting UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, EMI, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Can our e-commerce website handle thousands of products and high traffic surges?
Yes. We engineer e-commerce architectures with CDN asset distribution (ImageKit/Cloudflare), server-side database indexing, and caching mechanisms to ensure sub-second page loads even during peak festival flash sales.
Will our online store be mobile-friendly and fast on 4G/5G mobile networks?
Every e-commerce store we build follows strict mobile-first engineering with responsive touch gestures, sticky add-to-cart buttons, auto-compressed WebP images, and lightweight scripts for high Lighthouse performance scores.
Do you provide training on how to upload products and manage orders?
Yes. Upon completing development, we provide hands-on video training and administrative walkthroughs showing your team how to add products, adjust pricing, manage inventory, apply discount coupons, and process customer orders.
Can you integrate shipping aggregators and automatic delivery tracking?
Yes. We integrate popular courier and shipping aggregator APIs (such as Shiprocket, Delhivery, and India Post) to automate AWB generation, shipping label printing, and live tracking notifications for customers.
What security measures are implemented to protect customer payment data?
We implement end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption, PCI-DSS compliant payment tokenization (meaning credit card numbers never touch your server), database encryption, CSRF protection, and automated security audit protocols.
