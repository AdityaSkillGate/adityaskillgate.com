What Is Retail Billing Software?

Retail billing software is an integrated Point of Sale (POS) and inventory management platform designed to accelerate checkout counter operations, eliminate manual calculation mistakes, and keep exact real-time track of your store inventory.

Instead of relying on handwritten paper bills or slow generic spreadsheets, our software enables cashiers to scan barcodes, automatically calculate GST rates (CGST, SGST, IGST), process multiple payment modes (Cash, UPI, Card), and print itemized customer receipts in under 15 seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Retail Software FAQs

Clear answers regarding features, hardware setup, GST compliance, and deployment.

Is this software 100% compliant with Indian GST laws and HSN codes?
Yes. The billing software automatically calculates CGST, SGST, and IGST based on item tax slabs. You can assign HSN/SAC codes to products, print B2B tax invoices with buyer GSTIN, and export monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B summary reports for effortless tax filing.
Does the billing system require an active internet connection to work?
No. Our retail software includes dedicated offline desktop capability. Your cashiers can continue billing, scanning barcodes, and printing receipts seamlessly without internet. Data automatically synchronizes whenever connectivity is restored.
Can it connect with our existing barcode scanner, cash drawer, and thermal printer?
Yes. The software is compatible with all standard USB, Bluetooth, and wireless 1D/2D barcode scanners, electronic cash drawers, digital weighing scales, and 2-inch, 3-inch, or 4-inch ESC/POS thermal printers as well as standard A4 laser printers.
How does the software help track inventory and low-stock items?
Inventory is deducted in real time with every sale. Store owners can configure custom minimum reorder thresholds for each product. When stock falls below the threshold, the system displays instant warning alerts and generates automated reorder purchase lists.
Can multiple cashiers use the software with separate login restrictions?
Yes. The software features granular role-based user management. You can create separate logins for Cashiers, Inventory Managers, and Store Admins, restricting cashiers from editing product prices, giving unauthorized discounts, or accessing confidential business profit metrics.
What are the minimum computer hardware requirements to run the software?
The software is lightweight and runs smoothly on standard retail PC setups: Intel Core i3 (or equivalent), 4GB RAM, 64GB SSD/HDD, running Windows 10 or Windows 11. It also supports touch-screen POS terminals.
What kind of training, installation, and after-sales support do you provide?
We provide complete end-to-end setup including initial product database import, hardware integration (printer/scanner), staff training on the billing counter, and direct phone/WhatsApp technical support for troubleshooting.
