Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about admissions, batches, and placement support.

What is your typical response time?
We typically respond to all enquiries within 24 hours. For urgent matters, WhatsApp us directly at +91 63826 04808 for an immediate response.
Do you offer free career counselling?
Yes! We offer 100% free one-on-one career counselling to help you evaluate your academic background, choose the ideal course (Full Stack Web Development, Python AI/ML, Cloud, Digital Marketing), and plan your career roadmap.
Can I visit your training center in Sankarankovil?
Absolutely! We welcome walk-in visitors and prospective students at our state-of-the-art facility in Sankarankovil, Tamil Nadu. Please call or message us before visiting so our team can schedule your orientation.
Are training batches available online or offline?
We offer flexible learning modes: 100% live interactive online classes, in-person classroom batches at Sankarankovil, and hybrid options tailored to working professionals and college students with recorded revision access.
How does your 100% placement support work?
Our placement cell assists you from day one with resume building, portfolio creation on GitHub, mock technical and HR interviews, aptitude coaching, and direct interview drives with our 50+ hiring partner companies.
