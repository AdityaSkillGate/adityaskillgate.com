Unified Control for Modern Food Outlets

Running a successful food and beverage business requires seamless synchronization between front-of-house service, kitchen staff, stock rooms, and accounting.

Our Restaurant POS and Inventory platform unifies table management, instant Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) routing, ingredient-level consumption tracking, and daily cash drawer reconciliation into one unified system with full offline capability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Restaurant POS FAQs

Common questions from restaurant owners, cafe operators, and cloud kitchen managers.

Does the software support Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) printing?
Yes. As soon as a waiter or cashier punches an order, the system instantly transmits a Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) to the designated kitchen printer with table number, item modifiers, timestamps, and special cooking instructions.
Can the restaurant POS operate offline during internet interruptions?
Yes. Our restaurant POS is designed with dedicated offline local network capability. Order taking, table switching, KOT printing, and customer bill settlements continue uninterrupted without an active internet connection.
How does ingredient-level raw material inventory tracking work?
You can link menu recipes to raw ingredient items (e.g., 1 Chicken Biryani consumes 200g chicken, 150g basmati rice, 30ml oil). Each time a dish is billed, the corresponding raw ingredient quantities are automatically deducted from your live inventory.
What hardware devices are compatible with the software?
The system works seamlessly with standard Windows desktop PCs, touch-screen POS all-in-one terminals, Android tablets, 2-inch and 3-inch ESC/POS thermal receipt printers, electronic cash drawers, and network LAN/Wi-Fi kitchen printers.
Can waiters take orders on tablets or mobile phones?
Yes. The system supports mobile/tablet order taking. Captains and service staff can take table-side orders directly on handheld devices, which instantly route KOTs to the kitchen and update the central cashier screen.
Can I manage both Dine-In and Takeaway orders in the same system?
Yes. The POS dashboard provides dedicated workflows for Dine-In table billing (with split bills and table transfers), Fast-Checkout Takeaway counters, and Online Delivery aggregator order tracking.
How long does it take to install the software and train our staff?
Standard setup, menu configuration, and printer integration take just 1 to 2 days. Our team provides complete on-site or guided remote training for your cashiers, managers, and kitchen staff.
