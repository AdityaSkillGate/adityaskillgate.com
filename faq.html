Courses & Training

About Our Courses

What courses do you offer?

We offer job-oriented IT courses including Full Stack Web Development (React + Node.js), Python & AI/ML, Android Development, Cloud Computing (AWS/Azure), Digital Marketing, and UI/UX Design. All courses come with hands-on projects and placement support.

Are the courses available online or only offline?

We offer both online (live classes via Zoom/Google Meet) and offline (in-center) training. You can choose the mode that best suits your schedule and preference. Recorded sessions are also available for revision.

What are the course fees and duration?

Course fees range from ₹8,000 to ₹20,000 depending on the program. Duration varies from 1 to 6 months. We also offer EMI options and scholarship discounts for deserving students. Visit our Courses page for current pricing.

Do I get a certificate after completing the course?

Yes! Upon completing the course and projects, you receive an industry-recognized certificate from Aditya Skill Gate IT Solution. This certificate is valued by our hiring partner companies during recruitment.

What is the minimum qualification to enroll?

Most courses are open to anyone with basic computer knowledge. No prior coding experience is required for beginner courses. For advanced programs (AI/ML, Cloud), a basic understanding of programming is helpful but not mandatory.

How are classes scheduled?

We offer flexible batch timings: Morning (9 AM – 12 PM), Afternoon (2 PM – 5 PM), and Evening (6 PM – 9 PM) batches. Weekend-only batches are also available for working professionals.

Placement Support

Jobs & Placements

Do you guarantee 100% placement?

We have a 90%+ placement rate and provide dedicated placement support for all students who complete the course. While no institute can guarantee placement (it depends on individual performance), we do everything possible to help you get placed — including resume building, mock interviews, and direct referrals to + companies.

Which companies have hired your students?

Our students have been placed at TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Zoho, HCL, Cognizant, Accenture, Capgemini, IBM, Tech Mahindra, Hexaware, Mphasis, and many other top IT companies across India.

What is the highest salary package offered?

The highest package received by our students is ₹6 LPA. The average starting salary is ₹3–4 LPA for freshers. With 1-2 years of experience and continuous skill development, our alumni typically grow to ₹6–10+ LPA.

Can I upload my resume even if I haven't taken a course?

Yes! You can upload your resume on our Resume Upload page and our HR team will review it. If your profile matches our partner companies' requirements, we'll contact you. However, priority placement support is given to students who complete our courses.
